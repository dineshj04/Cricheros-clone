Our Pitch & Team

Who We Are

At CricMates, we're building the largest technology platform for grassroots cricket. We've reached over 42 million cricketers, scorers, umpires, and fans, but our vision is much larger. We want to make local cricket matches world-class and create a sustainable ecosystem for everyone involved in the game.

We are honest, humble, and ambitious. We empower every player to understand their match performance better and improve using data, insights, and digital recognition. Learn more at cricmates.html.

Who You Are

You think like a true all-rounder. Not just with bat and ball, but with mindset. You take ownership, step up under pressure, and refine your form with discipline and curiosity. You don't need to be the smartest person in the room; you just need hunger, responsibility, and the willingness to learn every single day.

Life at CricMates

The pitch changes constantly. Some days are smooth drives, other days test your core skills. We value open communication, context over control, and the creative freedom to test new ideas. Slack is our dugout, the office is our playground, and cricket is our daily culture.

  • Meaningful work responsibility
  • Freedom of flexible breaks
  • Freedom to query leadership
  • Constructive feedback channels
  • Creative playground to innovate
  • Structured growth paths

Open Spots

Ready to play a key role in our innings? Send your resume to people@cricmates.in.

Business Development All Rounder (North America) ▼

We're looking for a Business Development All-Rounder who is driven, analytical, and comfortable owning sales targets, partner communications, and international market insights.

Content & Copy Associate ▼

At CricMates, we want to tell stories that connect, inspire, and celebrate the spirit of grassroots cricket. You will lead copy and narrative operations for social, mail, and app screens.

BD All Rounder - CricMates Store ▼

The CricMates Store is our specialized e-commerce gear and custom merchandise vertical. You will manage merchant partnerships, logistics, and store sales operations.

Android Developer ▼

Our Android app is where millions of players score matches and analyze stats. You will design, build, and optimize high-performance features in Kotlin and Java.

Product Designer ▼

We are searching for a UI/UX Product Designer who can turn complex stats and scorecards into intuitive, gorgeous mobile and web app interactions.

iOS Developer ▼

Our iOS app serves as the main portal for Apple users to score local games. You will implement features, manage SDKs, and refactor code for speed in Swift.

Electronics Hardware Engineer ▼

We design IoT hardware to capture automated matches data. You will build, program, and bring up production-ready board designs and sensor modules.
