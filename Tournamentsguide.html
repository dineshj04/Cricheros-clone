Introduction

Whether you are organizing a cricket tournament for the first time or have done so multiple times, we all agree that it is a challenging job. Many questions arise in the mind of a cricket tournament organizer before taking the plunge, like:

  • How will I source local teams, umpires, scorers, and commentators?
  • How do I securely book and pay for grounds?
  • How can I convince corporate sponsors to fund my matches?
  • Is there an application that can streamline organizing local leagues?

CricMates is built specifically to address these challenges, simplifying tournament logistics so you can focus on the sport.

Groundwork

Firstly, you need to do some groundwork before going to the main steps. Decide the dates and determine what kind of tournament you are going to hold:

Based upon ball type: Tennis ball, Leather ball, or Tape ball tournament.

Tournament style: Knockout matches, Round-robin League, Super-Sixes, etc.

Tournament category:

  • Age dependent tournament (U-16, U-19, etc.)
  • Corporate Cricket leagues
  • Box Cricket or indoor tournament
  • Open Cricket Tournament

For internal corporate tournaments, funding is usually simple. For open tournaments, securing sponsors is key. Contact sports-related local brands, outline your marketing reach, and pitching online visibility on scorecard apps will yield results.

Venue & Match Officials

Selecting a suitable cricket ground takes up a good amount of time as you have to compare pitches, facilities, and costings.

CricMates has got you covered here. You will be able to see verified Cricket Grounds near you with details of booking costs, matches played, photos, and player reviews. Everything is available on a single portal so you can compare and contact owners directly.

To access grounds: Go to the community section from the navigation bar on CricMates, select Grounds, and you will see all listings in your vicinity.

A Scoring Platform

Broadcasting tournament match scores is critical for engagement. There are two primary routes:

Offline Scoring (Paper-Based)

Offline scoring is the traditional way, but it has severe drawbacks: no live updates for fans, risk of physical damage to score sheets, error-prone entries, and lack of permanent career records for local players.

Online Scoring (Digital)

Broadcasting scorecards LIVE ball-by-ball via smartphones is now the gold standard. Benefits include permanent player records, real-time fan engagement, and increased online branding slots for sponsors.

With over 8.7 Lakhs+ tournaments and more than 4.2 Crores+ players registered, CricMates is the world's most trusted grassroots scoring platform.

Tournament Registration

Setting up your tournament on the platform allows teams to register online and helps you collect entry fees systematically:

  • Select "Add A Tournament" option from the menu.
  • Fill details such as tournament name, ground, and category.
  • Add participating teams and designate match officials (umpires/scorers).
  • Define match schedule, brackets, and rules.
  • Specify entry registration fees and custom prizes.

Tournament Promotion

Promoting your tournament is crucial for player registration and spectator turnout. Leverage local social media groups, cricket WhatsApp circles, and CricMates community pages to market your schedule. With digital flyers showing dates, cash prizes, and ground locations, you can quickly fill team slots.

Live Streaming

Adding live video broadcasts makes your local tournament look professional. CricMates allows you to stream matches directly using a smartphone connected to a tripod on the boundary. Fans can watch HD live video complete with automatic overlays showing runs, wickets, overs, and batsman names, matching TV broadcast styles.

Recognize Sponsors

Sponsors want return on investment. With CricMates, you can sell premium digital banner ads, offer scorecard sponsorship, and display logo watermarks on your live streams. By offering automated sponsors slots, corporate partners see direct value, making it easier to secure funding.

Personalized Mobile App

CricMates builds white-labeled custom apps featuring your association logo, custom colors, match schedules, points tables, player rankings, and dedicated sponsor banners published on the Google Play Store and iOS App Store.
